## Current hiring-market signals

The `Role Intelligence` and `Sources` sheets inside `career-assets/Governance_Evidence_Matrix.xlsx` contain current vacancy examples and source URLs. The recurring signals reflected in the career materials are:

- GRC / information-security governance;
- ISO 27001 and SOC 2 control/evidence work;
- risk assessments, risk registers, remediation and exceptions;
- third-party/vendor risk;
- customer security assurance/questionnaires;
- policy/standards/documentation;
- AI governance, including NIST AI RMF / ISO 42001 / EU AI Act in relevant roles;
- technical/cloud/scripting fluency in technically oriented roles;
- GRC-platform and real audit-cycle experience in many more experienced roles.

Years-of-experience thresholds and direct audit ownership are treated as real blockers where the vacancy states them; they are not solved by changing the resume title.
