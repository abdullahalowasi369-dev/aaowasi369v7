## Positioning principle

The portfolio should look mature because the reasoning is mature, not because it claims a senior job title or fabricated tenure. The website leads with operating architecture, evidence lineage, exceptions, residual risk and decisions. The resume remains conventional and ATS-safe. The workbooks let a skeptical reviewer inspect scope and claim boundaries.

## Role-band strategy

The package is designed to compete most strongly for Technology Risk, GRC, Security Compliance, TPRM, Customer Assurance and AI Governance roles whose stated mandatory experience is compatible with the candidate's verified history.

More senior roles can still be evaluated individually, but explicit requirements such as 5+ years, end-to-end external audit ownership, formal program ownership, named GRC-platform administration or jurisdiction-specific work authorization remain actual gaps unless later evidenced.
